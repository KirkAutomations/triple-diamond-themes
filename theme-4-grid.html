How It Works

Assessment

You tell us about the property. We walk it, evaluate the market, and give you a management plan with realistic numbers. No pitch deck—just the situation and what we'd do about it.

Onboarding

We handle tenant placement, lease execution, and the operational handoff. You get portal access from day one so nothing is a black box.

Ongoing Management

Rent collection, maintenance coordination, lease enforcement, financial reporting. We answer the phone when tenants call and when you call. That's the whole point.

FAQ

What areas do you cover?
Macomb, Oakland, and Wayne counties. Most of our portfolio is in Shelby Township, Sterling Heights, Warren, Troy, Rochester Hills, and Detroit.
How much does management cost?
It depends on the property. We'll give you a straight number after we see the place—not a range designed to get you on a call.
I already have tenants. Can you take over?
Yes. We review the existing lease, meet the tenants, and transition management without disrupting occupancy. It's a common starting point.
