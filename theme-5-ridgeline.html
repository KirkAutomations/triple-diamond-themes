How it works

What you bring

The address, the situation, and what you need from a management company. We don’t start with a sales pitch—we start with the property. Market data, condition assessment, and a management plan that fits the actual asset.

What stays secure

Your financials, tenant records, and property documents live in a secure owner portal. Monthly statements, year-end reporting, and maintenance history—accessible anytime, no phone call required.

Who answers

A real person in Shelby Township. Not a call center, not a chatbot, not a voicemail that never gets returned. You get a direct line to the person managing your property.

Common Questions

We manage residential properties throughout Macomb, Oakland, and Wayne counties. Our office is in Shelby Township, and most of our portfolio is within a 30-mile radius—close enough to respond quickly when something needs attention.

We pull comparable rental data for the specific address, factor in the property’s condition and features, and look at current market velocity. The goal is a price that fills the unit quickly with a qualified tenant—not a number that sounds good but sits empty for three months.
